# Economy

Starting values for testing — not medical advice, not scientifically tuned.

| Mechanic | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Walking reward | 1,000 recorded steps → 1 discovery credit |
| Daily limit | 10 step-earned credits (self-reported routines share this cap) |
| Spending | 1 credit → 1 seed **or** 1 egg |
| Starter | 1 seed (Moon Maple) and 1 egg (Pearl Guppy), no permissions |
| Tutorial growth | 75 seconds to mature |
| Common / uncommon / rare | 6 / 24 / 48 growth-hours |
| Permanence | No death, decay, or confiscation |
| Visit bonus | First 30 foreground minutes/day: extra 1× (2× total). Then 1×. |

The ten-credit cap is game balancing, not a 10,000-step medical goal. Users pick their own walking goal; missing it does nothing to the world.

Do not sell steps, sleep, hatching speed, rescue medicine, or health permission. Charge later for themes, cosmetic collections, and a larger original sound library. Already-placed cosmetics remain if a subscription ends.
